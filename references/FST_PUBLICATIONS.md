# FST Research Publications Used by This Repository

This SSAC repository is restricted to the FST.ai / FST.ai 2.0 officiating research line.

## FST.ai

Keivan Shariatmadar and Ahmad Osman (2025). **AI-Enhanced Precision in Sport Taekwondo: Increasing Fairness, Speed, and Trust in Competition (FST.ai).** arXiv:2507.14657. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2507.14657.

Public record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14657

The paper introduces the FST.ai framework for AI-assisted Taekwondo officiating, with particular emphasis on real-time head-kick detection and scoring support using computer vision, deep learning, pose/motion information, impact-related analysis, and edge inference.

## FST.ai 2.0

Keivan Shariatmadar, Ahmad Osman, Ramin Ray, Usman Dildar, and Kisam Kim (2025). **FST.ai 2.0: An Explainable AI Ecosystem for Fair, Fast, and Inclusive Decision-Making in Olympic and Paralympic Taekwondo.** arXiv:2510.18193. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2510.18193.

Public record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18193

The current arXiv metadata is reproduced here as the bibliographic source of record. In particular, it lists the third author's surname as **Ray**. The SSAC submission identifies **Ramin Rey** according to the author's current/preferred spelling supplied for that submission. This repository does not silently alter the authorship metadata of the prior publication.

FST.ai 2.0 extends the research with pose-based action recognition, epistemic-uncertainty modeling using credal sets, and explainability mechanisms. Its public abstract reports an 85% reduction in decision-review time and 93% referee trust in AI-assisted decisions. Those reported figures are treated as claims from the prior public record until the SSAC evidence audit links them to their evaluation population, denominator, protocol, and releasable supporting artifacts.
