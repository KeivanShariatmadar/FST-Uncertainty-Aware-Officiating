# Data Statement

## 1. Scope

This statement describes the data status for the SSAC study on uncertainty-aware AI-assisted video review in competitive Taekwondo.

## 2. Source material

The FST research uses competition-video evidence and associated officiating information for studying difficult scoring actions, including head-kick review. Some underlying material may contain identifiable athletes, officials, event branding, broadcast material, or other content controlled by third parties.

## 3. Rights and privacy constraints

Public possession of a research result does not imply that the authors own redistribution rights to every underlying video frame or recording. Source footage will therefore **not** be placed in this repository unless its redistribution rights, privacy basis, and relevant permissions have been verified.

Restrictions may arise from athlete privacy and likeness rights, event or federation rights, broadcasting rights, contractual obligations, or other third-party rights.

## 4. What may be released

Subject to provenance and rights review, the authors intend to release research artifacts sufficient to support independent scrutiny of reported results where legally permissible. These may include:

- de-identified or non-identifying event/action records;
- ground-truth or expert labels where release is permitted;
- model outputs used in evaluation;
- review-latency observations;
- uncertainty or confidence outputs;
- aggregate contingency tables and summary statistics;
- data dictionaries; and
- scripts that reproduce reported metrics from the released derived data.

## 5. What is not currently released

At Stage 1, this repository does not publish raw competition video, proprietary production data, production model weights, confidential deployment logs, or unverified numerical datasets.

The absence of restricted source footage must not be interpreted as a claim that the source footage is open data.

## 6. Quantitative-claim provenance

The SSAC abstract draws on the public FST.ai / FST.ai 2.0 research record. Before a full-paper submission, each quantitative claim will be linked to its underlying evaluation population, denominator, measurement protocol, and releasable evidence artifact where available.

## 7. Access questions

Questions about whether a specific research artifact can be shared should be directed to the corresponding author. Access cannot be promised where the authors do not control the relevant third-party rights.
